About me

I'm Jethro Almodiel, a Computer Science student, aspiring software engineer, and young entrepreneur with a passion for technology, innovation, and turning ideas into reality. I enjoy challenging myself by building real-world solutions, from developing software applications to exploring artificial intelligence and creating systems that can make people's lives easier. As the founder of XIMO, I'm taking my first steps into entrepreneurship while continuously improving my skills in programming, leadership, and business. I've also had the opportunity to compete in hackathons, earn recognition for my projects, and share my knowledge with aspiring developers. I believe that growth comes from constantly learning, taking risks, and stepping outside my comfort zone. My goal isn't just to become a successful developer, but to build something meaningful, inspire others, and prove that age doesn't define what you can achieve.

I enjoy exploring web development, solving problems, and continuously learning to become a better developer. My goal is to create websites that are both visually appealing and easy to use.
